# THE WOODSMAN - Series Bible

**Genre:** Post-Apocalyptic LitRPG / Dark Fantasy  
**Tone:** Dark, gritty, brutal with moments of hope  
**Target Audience:** Adult readers who enjoy hard survival stories and LitRPG mechanics  

---

## CORE PREMISE

When China sabotages US nuclear reactors causing a global nuclear holocaust, an AI entity named Zero intervenes to contain the radiation using nanorobots. After accidentally nearly killing former Green Beret Dean "The Woodsman" Jacobs, she asks him what he wants his world to look like. His sarcastic response - "What, this new hellscape isn't already enough like a survival RPG?" - is taken literally by Zero, who implements brutal RPG mechanics powered by radiation and unleashes literal demons from other dimensions onto Earth before leaving to handle other multiversal matters.

Now Dean and all of humanity must suffer radiation poisoning to gain power, fight interdimensional demons, and survive in a world where Zero's "gift" may have doomed them all.

### The System (RPG Mechanics)

**System Interface:**
- Personalized to individual's visualization style
- Visual thinkers: HUD, AR-style overlay
- Non-visual thinkers: Auditory prompts or VR-style screens
- Customized to mental processing preferences

**Status Display Includes:**
- HP (Health Points)
- Radiation (functions as mana/energy pool)
- Stats: Strength, Speed, Reflexes, Recovery, Radiation
- Level
- Abilities
- Status effects

**Core Mechanics:**

**LEVELING/POWER ACQUISITION - THE COST:**
- Must expose oneself to radiation to gain experience/power
- Suffer REAL radiation poisoning effects:
  - Acute Radiation Syndrome symptoms
  - Nausea, vomiting
  - Radiation burns (skin damage, blistering)
  - Hair loss
  - Internal bleeding
  - Cellular damage
  - Organ stress
  - Extreme pain
- Nanobots heal damage enough to prevent death
- **The horror:** To become powerful, you must endure cancer-level suffering repeatedly
- **Active method:** Deliberately expose yourself to hot zones
- **Passive method:** Ambient radiation in wasteland slowly levels you up (slower, less painful)
- **The trap:** Need power to survive demons, but gaining power nearly kills you

**Stats Explained:**
- **Strength:** Physical power, carrying capacity, melee damage
- **Speed:** Movement speed, combat speed
- **Reflexes:** Reaction time, dodge capability, accuracy
- **Recovery:** Healing rate, stamina regeneration, resistance to status effects
- **Radiation:** Storage capacity for radiation energy
  - Functions as mana pool for abilities
  - Can be depleted by using powers
  - **CRITICAL:** If radiation drops too low, you LOSE A LEVEL
  - Creates risk/reward: use power now or save it to maintain level?

**Abilities:**
- NOT magic - radiation-based powers (think Godzilla)
- Examples: Radiation blasts, enhanced regeneration, energy projection, radiation manipulation
- Powered by Radiation stat (mana pool)
- Can gain new abilities through leveling or specific achievements
- Dean's first ability: **Regeneration (Passive)** - gained while being burned alive by demon worm mucus

**Using Abilities:**
- Does NOT cause pain
- Drains Radiation stat
- Risk of losing level if Radiation depleted too much

**Leveling Up:**
- Does NOT cause pain
- Increases base stats
- May unlock new abilities
- Increases Radiation capacity
